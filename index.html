I. Establishing Your Coaching Approach

Required Reading (in Coaching Flag Football)

  • Chapter 1 Learning and Leading in Flag Football
  • Chapter 2 Coaching Philosophy and Priorities
  • Chapter 3 Communication and Motivation

Learning Objectives

Chapter 1
  • Explain the growth of flag football and the expanding role of coaches in developing the sport.
  • Differentiate flag football from tackle football and apply those distinctions to coaching decisions and athlete development.
  • Recognize the leadership responsibilities of a flag football coach in creating positive athletic experiences and advancing the sport.
Chapter 2
  • Develop a personal coaching philosophy that guides decision-making, player development, and program leadership.
  • Promote athlete growth by creating opportunities to build life skills, resilience, and positive experiences through sport.
  • Apply the principles of hard work, continuous learning, and simplicity to maximize coaching effectiveness and player performance.
Chapter 3
  • Demonstrate effective communication strategies that strengthen relationships, enhance learning, and support team success.
  • Apply communication techniques appropriate to practices, games, and interactions with parents and stakeholders.
  • Create an environment that fosters player motivation, confidence, accountability, and self-improvement.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify the three primary coaching objectives—winning, athlete enjoyment, and athlete development—and explain how they influence coaching decisions.
  • Apply the philosophy of “Athletes First, Winning Second” when making coaching decisions that affect athlete welfare and performance.
  • Recognize the characteristics, advantages, and challenges associated with early- and late-maturing athletes.
  • Apply coaching strategies that support equitable athlete development regardless of physical maturity, including avoiding early specialization and position pigeonholing.
  • Apply a positive communication approach that promotes athlete confidence, learning, and motivation while avoiding negative communication habits.
  • Use clear, specific, and information-rich feedback to improve athlete performance and understanding.

II. Building and Maintaining a Program

Required Reading (in Coaching Flag Football)

  • Chapter 4 Coach a Team, Construct a Program
  • Chapter 5 A Culture of Caring
  • Chapter 6 Program Support and Resources
  • Chapter 7 Program Organization and Expectations
  • Chapter 8 Season and Practice Scheduling

Learning Objectives

Chapter 4
  • Differentiate between building a team and building a sustainable program that supports long-term success.
  • Establish and reinforce a repeatable program philosophy that emphasizes standards, athlete development, and continuity.
  • Develop players and feeder systems that strengthen both current team performance and future program growth.
Chapter 5
  • Explain how a culture of caring contributes to athlete development, team cohesion, and program success.
  • Apply standards of behavior that promote positive conduct, cohesion, sacrifice, gratitude, and respect within a team.
  • Utilize team leadership and captains to sustain and strengthen program culture.
Chapter 6
  • Identify the personnel, facilities, equipment, and community resources necessary to support a successful flag football program.
  • Build productive relationships with assistant coaches, managers, parents, school personnel, and game staff.
  • Organize and manage program resources to enhance player experiences and overall program effectiveness.
Chapter 7
  • Organize and manage a flag football program by effectively delegating responsibilities, aligning stakeholders, and maintaining operational cohesion.
  • Establish and communicate clear expectations for players, coaches, parents, and supporters that promote accountability, commitment, and program success.
  • Apply leadership principles that foster adaptability, teamwork, character, and a unified culture throughout the program.
Chapter 8
  • Design a seasonal plan that aligns practice, competition, and player development goals throughout the year.
  • Organize phase-specific practices that support skill development, team culture, and competitive readiness.
  • Apply effective scheduling strategies to maximize player participation, staff effectiveness, and team performance.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify factors within sport that can contribute to mental health concerns and understand the coach’s role in supporting athlete well-being.
  • Implement athlete-protection practices, communication guidelines, and professional boundaries that help create a safe and respectful sport environment.
  • Identify the causes, symptoms, and consequences of athlete burnout and implement practices that reduce risk.
  • Demonstrate self-awareness and emotional regulation strategies that enable coaches to model effective anxiety and stress management during competition.
  • Apply the five-step instruction planning process: identifying required skills, knowing athletes, analyzing the coaching situation, establishing priorities, and planning practices.
  • Design developmentally appropriate, purposeful, and engaging practice sessions that maximize learning and active participation.

III. Teaching the Game to the Team

Required Reading (in Coaching Flag Football)

  • Chapter 9 Developing Offensive Skills
  • Chapter 10 Playing Offense
  • Chapter 11 Developing Defensive Skills
  • Chapter 12 Playing Defense
  • Chapter 13 Coaching Special Teams

Learning Objectives

Chapter 9
  • Teach and develop fundamental offensive skills, including receiving, route running, and running back and quarterback play.
  • Apply effective instruction, feedback, and drills to support skill acquisition, refinement, and confident performance.
  • Evaluate quarterback technical, tactical, and mental skills to enhance offensive execution and player development.
Chapter 10
  • Design and implement an offensive system that aligns with team strengths and quarterback capabilities.
  • Apply principles of play calling, route running, and quarterback decision-making to improve offensive efficiency.
  • Organize offensive formations, practice methods, and film study to promote consistent execution and performance.
Chapter 11
  • Develop defensive fundamentals, including aggression, flag-pulling technique, and position-specific skills.
  • Apply drills and coaching strategies that improve defensive execution and player confidence.
  • Evaluate player traits to assign defensive positions that maximize team performance.
Chapter 12
  • Explain the core defensive principles that guide successful team defense.
  • Compare the strengths, weaknesses, and responsibilities of common defensive schemes, including Cover 2, Cover 3, and man-to-man defense.
  • Implement defensive alignments and adjustments based on down, distance, opponent tendencies, and quarterback capabilities.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply the four-step process for teaching technical skills: introducing the skill, demonstrating and explaining the skill, providing practice opportunities, and correcting errors through feedback.
  • Design practice activities that promote skill acquisition through game-like conditions, efficient organization, appropriate progressions, and enjoyable learning experience.
  • Apply the four-step risk management process by identifying risks, evaluating their likelihood and severity, selecting appropriate risk-management strategies, and implementing those strategies.
  • Develop procedures that protect athletes from harm, including those for injury prevention, concussion management, bullying prevention, and emergency preparedness.

IV. Preparing for and Coaching Games

Required Reading (in Coaching Flag Football)

  • Chapter 14 Off-Season Goals and Competitions
  • Chapter 15 Game Planning and Preparation
  • Chapter 16 Sportsmanship
  • Chapter 17 In-Game Adjustments
  • Chapter 18 Postgame Review

Learning Objectives

Chapter 14
  • Identify the three primary objectives of the off-season: culture, player development, and program standards.
  • Design off-season activities that promote player growth while reinforcing team culture and routines.
  • Evaluate the purpose of off-season competitions and align them with developmental goals rather than win-loss outcomes.
Chapter 15
  • Develop and implement a structured game plan that prepares players for specific opponents.
  • Analyze game film and scouting information to identify opponent tendencies and team performance needs.
  • Apply a consistent preparation process that builds player confidence, understanding, and readiness for competition.
Chapter 16
  • Explain the role of respect as the foundation of sportsmanship in flag football.
  • Promote respectful interactions with officials, opponents, coaches, and the game to enhance program culture and reputation.
  • Demonstrate strategies for modeling and teaching positive sportsmanship to players, parents, and fans.
Chapter 17
  • Recognize when in-game adjustments are needed and differentiate between major changes and minor tactical tweaks.
  • Develop players’ ability to self-correct, communicate, and adapt under game pressure.
  • Apply effective communication strategies to implement offensive and defensive adjustments during competition.
Chapter 18
  • Evaluate game performance against pregame objectives to identify areas of success and improvement.
  • Implement a structured postgame process that reinforces sportsmanship, reflection, and accountability.
  • Use postgame meetings and feedback to support player development and prepare for future competition.
